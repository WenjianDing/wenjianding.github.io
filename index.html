About

I am a Specially Appointed Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Health Data Science at Juntendo University, supervised by Prof. Zhe Sun in the Himeno-Sun Lab. I received my Ph.D. from Nankai University under the supervision of Prof. Zhenglu Yang and previously worked as a research student at Juntendo University.

My work focuses on multimodal understanding and question-answer generation. My recent research interests focus on Reliable Diagnostic Reasoning and Multimodal Clinical Evidence Integration. I am looking for collaborators in these areas.

Research interests

  • Reliable Diagnostic Reasoning
  • Multimodal Clinical Evidence Integration
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Multimodal Question Answering

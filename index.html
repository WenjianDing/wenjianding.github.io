About

I am a Specially Appointed Assistant Professor at Juntendo University. My research focuses on medical report generation, natural language processing, and multimodal question answering.

I study how to generate and evaluate questions, answers, and distractors grounded in images and videos. I also work on radiology report generation and structured retrieval-augmented generation for radiology impression inference.

My doctoral research at Nankai University was supervised by Prof. Zhenglu Yang. I was also a research student at Juntendo University's Graduate School of Health Data Science, working with Prof. Zhe Sun.
